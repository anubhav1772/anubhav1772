My areas of interest include robotics, reinforcement learning, and learning-based control, with a particular focus on legged robotic systems, including quadrupeds and humanoids. My research has primarily focused on energy-efficient locomotion, adaptive gait control, fall recovery, and robust sim-to-real transfer. 

I have experience with physics simulation environments such as Isaac Gym, MuJoCo, and PyBullet, along with ROS 2 for robotic software development, simulation, and system integration. I am currently expanding my simulation and learning-based control workflows toward NVIDIA Isaac Lab and Isaac Sim, particularly for humanoid robotics and sim-to-real applications.

### Current Research Directions

My broader research interests include:

- **Model-based reinforcement learning**
- **Learned world models**
- **Uncertainty-aware control**
- **Imitation learning**
- **Generative action policies**, including diffusion and flow matching
- **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models**
- **Multimodal foundation models**

I am particularly interested in predictive and adaptive control frameworks that use 
learned dynamics and uncertainty estimation to anticipate instability, detect unfamiliar 
operating conditions, and enable proactive adaptation or recovery.
